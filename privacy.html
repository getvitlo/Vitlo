Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy explains what Vitlo collects, why, and what control you have over it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who we are

Vitlo is operated by Hani Hasan, based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy question or request, contact trustforge.hani@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Profile information — the name, age, sex, height, weight and optional body fat percentage you enter, used to calculate your metabolic rate and calorie targets.
  • Food and exercise logs — the meals you record (name, calories, macronutrients) and workouts you log.
  • Goal settings — your chosen goal and daily targets.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing activity, or advertising identifiers. Vitlo contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Food photographs

When you photograph a meal, the image is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for nutritional analysis and the result is returned to your device. Vitlo does not store your food photographs. Only the resulting text (meal name, calories, macros) is saved to your log.

How your data is stored

Your account and logs are stored using Supabase, an encrypted cloud database provider. Access is restricted so that each account can only read and write its own records. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Why we process your data

We process your information solely to provide the service you signed up for: calculating your targets, storing your logs, and generating personalised coaching responses. Under UK GDPR our lawful basis is performance of a contract with you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Vitlo Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card details. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier only.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, or a copy of your data in a portable format. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Menu → Settings → Delete my account. This is immediate and permanent. Alternatively, email us and we will action any request within 30 days.

Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, food logs and workout records are erased immediately and permanently.

Children

Vitlo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Health disclaimer

Vitlo provides general nutritional information and estimates. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional. Calorie and macronutrient figures produced by AI analysis are estimates and may be inaccurate. If you have a medical condition, an eating disorder, or are pregnant, speak to your GP before changing your diet.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

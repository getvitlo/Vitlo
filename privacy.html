Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026UK GDPR

This explains exactly what Vitlo collects, why it needs it, and what control you have. It is written to be read, not skipped.

The short version

  • We collect your email, the body stats you enter, and the meals and workouts you log.
  • Food photographs are analysed and then discarded — we never store them.
  • We don't sell your data, show you adverts, or use your information to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, instantly.

Who we are

Vitlo is operated by Hani Hasan, based in the United Kingdom, and is the data controller for the information described here. For any privacy question or request, email trustforge.hani@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Email address
Creates your account and signs you in. Nothing else.
Body stats
Name, age, sex, height, weight and optional body fat percentage — used to calculate your metabolic rate and daily calorie target.
Food logs
Meal names, calories and macronutrients, so your history and totals work.
Workouts
Calories burned and step counts you enter, to offset against your daily target.
Goal settings
Your chosen goal and targets.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your browsing activity, or advertising identifiers. Vitlo contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Your food photographs

WORTH KNOWING

When you photograph a meal, the image is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for nutritional analysis and the result comes back to your device. Vitlo does not store your photographs. Only the resulting text — meal name, calories, macros — is saved to your log.

How your data is stored

Your account and logs are held in Supabase, an encrypted cloud database. Access rules mean each account can only ever read and write its own records. Everything travels over encrypted connections.

Our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR we process your information on the basis of performance of a contract — you asked us to track your nutrition, and this is the data required to do that. We do not process your data for any other purpose.

What we never do

  • Sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • Share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Use it to train AI models.
  • Build advertising profiles about you.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Vitlo Pro, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card details. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and nothing more.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can request access to your data, correction of anything inaccurate, deletion of everything, or a portable copy.

DELETE EVERYTHING, ANY TIME

Open the app, tap the menu, then Settings → Delete my account. Your profile, meals and workouts are erased immediately and permanently. No email required, no waiting period.

You can also email us and we will action any request within 30 days. If you believe we have handled your data improperly, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is erased at the same moment — we do not keep a copy.

Children

Vitlo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Health information

Vitlo provides general nutritional estimates. It is not medical advice and not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional. Figures produced by AI analysis are estimates and can be inaccurate. If you have a medical condition, a history of disordered eating, or you are pregnant, speak to your GP before changing your diet.

Changes to this policy

If anything material changes we will update the date at the top and, where it matters, tell you in the app.

What Vitlo is

Vitlo is a calorie and macronutrient tracker. It uses AI to estimate the nutritional content of meals from photographs or written descriptions, and gives coaching guidance based on the information you enter.

Estimates, not measurements

READ THIS ONE

Nutritional figures in Vitlo are AI estimates. They are not laboratory measurements and they will sometimes be wrong. Do not rely on them for medical or clinical purposes. You can correct any result inside the app, and entering the food's weight noticeably improves accuracy.

Not medical advice

Vitlo does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and its coaching content is general information only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise — particularly if you have a medical condition, a history of disordered eating, or you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

Most trackers fold exercise into one multiplier and then let you add workouts on top, which counts the same session twice and quietly closes your deficit. Vitlo separates the two: your activity level covers ordinary living, your burn goal covers training, and logging a workout replaces the assumption rather than stacking on it.

What you get for the calories

A number on its own tells you very little. Vitlo is built around what the number means.

A health score on everything

Every meal is scored out of 100 on protein density, processing, added sugar and fibre, with a line on why. Two meals of the same calories are rarely the same meal.

A coach that reads the fortnight

It notices what a single day cannot: the meal that comes back three times a week, protein running short five days in seven. Then it says what that costs, in your numbers.

Lighter versions of the same meal

Not "eat less". Drop the mayo, swap the side, ask for it grilled — each with the calories it saves, offered before you log, while the food is still in front of you.

Barcodes and yesterday

Point at a barcode and it reads on its own. Forgot to log last night? Yesterday stays open, because meals get missed and remembered in the morning.

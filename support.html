FAQ

How do I scan food? Tap +, choose Photo or Describe, and the AI calculates your calories and macros.

How accurate is it? The AI uses standard nutritional values and portion estimation. You can correct any result.

How do I change my goal? Go to Settings and tap AI Coach.

Privacy Policy

What we collect: Your name, age, sex, weight, height, and nutrition goal when you create an account. Your food logs (meal names, calories, macros) and exercise data as you use the app. Your email address for account authentication.

Food photos: When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) for analysis. Photos are not stored permanently by Vitlo.

How we store it: Your data is stored securely using Supabase, an encrypted cloud database. Each user can only access their own data.

What we don't do: We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not use your data for purposes other than providing the Vitlo service.

Deleting your data: Contact trustforge.hani@gmail.com to request full deletion of your account and data.

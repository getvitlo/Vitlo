Vitlo is an AI-powered calorie and macro tracker that helps you reach your nutrition goals.

FAQ

How do I scan food? Tap the + button, choose Photo or Describe, and the AI will calculate your calories and macros.

How accurate is it? The AI uses standard nutritional databases and visual portion estimation. You can correct any result using the adjustment feature.

How do I change my goal? Go to Settings and tap AI Coach to set a new goal.

Privacy

Vitlo stores your food logs securely. We do not sell your data. Food photos are processed by AI and not stored permanently.

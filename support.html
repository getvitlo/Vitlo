Getting started

How do I log a meal?
Tap the green + button. Photograph your plate, describe what you ate in plain English, or enter the numbers manually. The AI identifies each item, estimates portions and calculates your macros.

How accurate is the AI?
It uses standard nutritional values and visual portion estimation, and gives each result a confidence level. It is an estimate, not a measurement. If something looks wrong, use "Not quite right? Tell the AI" to correct it — for example "it was 5 sausages not 4" — and it recalculates.

Can I add the food weight?
Yes, and it noticeably improves accuracy. Weigh your plate and enter the grams in the details section before analysing.

Targets and goals

How is my daily target calculated?
We calculate your basal metabolic rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation from your height, weight, age and sex, multiply it by an activity factor for maintenance, then apply your chosen deficit or surplus.

Can I set my own numbers?
Yes. Open Menu → Goals to change your goal or deficit, and to override the protein, carb and fat targets. If you know your exact BMR from a body scan, enter it under Menu → Profile.

Subscription

What does Vitlo Pro include?
Unlimited AI scans, full coach chat, health scores and complete history. Free accounts get three AI scans a day plus unlimited manual logging.

How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Vitlo. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Pro isn't showing.
Open Menu → Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

Account and data

How do I delete my account?
Menu → Settings → Delete my account. This erases your profile, meals and workouts immediately and permanently.

Do you store my food photos?
No. Photos are analysed and discarded. Only the resulting nutrition text is saved. See our Privacy Policy.

Something not working?

If a scan fails, check your connection and tap Retry. If it keeps failing, email us with a description of what you were logging and we'll investigate.

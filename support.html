Logging food

How do I log a meal?

Tap the green + button. Photograph your plate, describe what you ate in plain English, or enter the numbers yourself. The AI identifies each item, estimates portions, and calculates your macros.

How accurate is it?

It uses standard nutritional values and visual portion estimation, and gives each result a confidence level. It's an estimate, not a measurement. If something looks wrong, use Not quite right? Tell the AI — type something like "it was 5 sausages not 4" and it recalculates.

Can I improve accuracy?

Yes. Weigh your plate and enter the grams in the Details section. It makes a noticeable difference.

I've run out of scans

Free accounts get three AI scans a day, resetting at midnight. Manual entry is unlimited, and Vitlo Pro removes the scan limit entirely.

Targets and goals

How is my daily target worked out?

We calculate your basal metabolic rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation from your height, weight, age and sex, multiply it by an activity factor to get maintenance, then apply your chosen deficit or surplus.

Can I set my own numbers?

Yes. Menu → Goals lets you change your goal and deficit, and override the protein, carb and fat targets. If you know your exact BMR from a body scan, enter it under Menu → Profile and we'll use that instead of the estimate.

Exercise

Can I log more than one workout a day?

Yes. Each session is logged separately and they add up across the day. You can remove any individual session from the Exercise tab.

Subscription

What does Pro include?

Unlimited AI scans, full coach chat, health scores on every meal, and your complete history.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Vitlo. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I paid but Pro isn't showing

Open Menu → Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?

Menu → Settings → Delete my account. This erases your profile, meals and workouts immediately and permanently.

Do you store my food photos?

No. Photos are analysed and discarded — only the resulting nutrition text is saved. See our Privacy Policy.

Something not working?

If a scan fails, check your connection and tap Retry. If it keeps failing, email us with a description of what you were logging and we'll look into it.
